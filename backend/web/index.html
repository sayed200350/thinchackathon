How to use this workspace
  1. Upload a claims CSV and the policy (a Word .docx or JSON file). Check the rules shown from your policy document. Assessment starts once both are loaded.
  2. Select a claim to read its decision and try edited facts.
  3. Choose a hypothetical policy or upload a candidate JSON, then run the assessment.
  4. Download the seven-column CSV or the full report. Corrections to saved open claims are recorded separately.
Uploaded files and edits stay in this browser session and are lost on refresh. Reviewer corrections are saved locally in SQLite. Candidate policies are previews, not releases. The engine recommends handling steps; it never approves payment.
Independent accuracy is measured only by the labelled development regression command. Synthetic cases and candidate changes: independent quality not measured.
